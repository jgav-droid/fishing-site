Appendix A: 2026 Statewide Trout and Kokanee Stocking Plan (Does not include high lake fry and fingerling plants)

Stocking allotments and timing are subject to change without notice

Stocking Month: Exact dates are not known at this time. Stocking timing will be left to the convenience of the hatcheries.
Fish Species: CT=cutthroat trout, LCT=lahontan cutthroat BT=brown trout, EBT=eastern brook trout, TEBT = triploid eastern brook trout, RB=rainbow trout, TRB=triploid rainbow trout, KOK=kokanee, TT=tiger trout and GT=golden trout
Fishing Season: OD= 4th Saturday in April, YR=Year-Round ( regardless of the season listed it is the Anglers responsibility to check the "Fishing in Washington " pamphlet to assure compliance with the regulations.
Catchables: Trout that have been reared at WDFW facilities and are 2.0 to 2.5 fish per pound in size. ** Denotes sterile trout.
Put, Grow &Take: Trout that have been reared at WDFW facilities and are 2.6 to 10 fish per pound in size.
Jumbos: Trout that have been reared at WDFW facilities that weigh 1 pound or more each. Note: includes brood fish.
Triploids: Triploid trout that have been purchased by the WDFW. Each fish weighs about 1.5 pounds.
Fry/Fingerling: Trout or kokanee that have been reared at WDFW facilities that were released at 10 to 600 fish per pound. Trout fry/fingerlings released in 2025 will harvestable in 2026. Kokanee fry/fingerlings released in 2025 will be
harvestable in fall 2027/spring 2028.
